RESEARCH STORY AND PROPOSED TITLE
Suggested title: "FakeCT: Controlled Digital Sibling Populations for Coarctation-Focused AI Evaluation."
Core need: controllable, traceable, consistently labeled data with deliberate coverage of rare anatomical configurations for AI development. Frame inconsistent labeling and uneven disease coverage as challenges in assembling clinical CT datasets, rather than asserting that all patient datasets are small or biased.
Research hypothesis: anatomically diverse digital sibling populations may improve AI robustness on unseen anatomy and ultimately unseen clinical CT. This is the hypothesis, not an established outcome.
The methodological contribution is FakeCT's anatomy editing and reproducible data organization. The case study is CoA-like aortic morphology. The AI contribution is an image-based, coarse-to-fine 3D classifier and a rigorous grouped evaluation framework.
Authors, affiliations, funding, contact and any institutional logos remain clearly marked placeholders until supplied. Do not invent them.

Methods facts to verify against current code:
- Let M_before and M_after be Boolean target masks. Added = M_after AND NOT M_before; Removed = M_before AND NOT M_after. Do not apply OR/AND directly to integer tissue IDs or attenuation values.
- Initial targets use tissue categories, optional atlas-defined anatomical filters and named tube ROIs. The selection ROI identifies the original target; permitted growth can extend beyond it into a bounded edit envelope.
- Dilation proposes growth along physical, six-face-neighbor paths. Eligibility and dimensionless tissue resistance limit borrowing. Accepted voxels copy the original anatomical ID and attenuation proxy of the winning target seed.
- Erosion releases target voxels; eligible neighboring tissue seeds propagate through the proposed release region. Physical path length plus configured resistance determines ownership. Equal-cost ties use signed original label ID and seed index. Copy the winning recipient's original ID and attenuation proxy. This is not a majority vote.
- Unresolved releases are preserved and logged. Optional local-volume/connectivity safeguards can back off excessive erosion. Resistance factors are not experimentally measured biomechanical stiffness.
- Accepted edits update the label-coded anatomical ACT volume and ATN attenuation proxy. Do not describe ACT as newly synthesized physiological radioactive activity, or ATN as reconstructed patient CT/Hounsfield units. Current dataset images are attenuation-copy proxies in cm^-1; original source volumes remain intact.
